Who We Are

Mbuzi Choma is a local restaurant specializing in authentic Ugandan grilled, boiled and fried goat meat. We serve delicious meals prepared over charcoal and oven to give you the unforgettable seasoned goat meat taste.

Our Mission

To bring families and friends together over great food and excellent customer service.

Thank you for choosing Uganda.
